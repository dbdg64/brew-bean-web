About Brew & Bean

A small cafe with a big, cozy heart.

We started in 2022 as two college friends who loved coffee and chai. Today, we're a small neighborhood cafe where anyone can come in, sit down, and feel at home.

We make fresh food during the day, brew every cup to order, and keep our prices affordable and homey, no fancy stuff, just good vibes.

Students come here to study, young workers take a happy break, and families gather on weekends. Everyone is welcome.
